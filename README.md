# DMO Anti-VEGF Regimens

An interactive comparison of anti-VEGF dosing strategies for diabetic macular oedema (DMO). It sets the DRCR Retina Network modified PRN (as-needed) algorithm against the dosing regimens used in the phase 3 registration trials.

**Live page:** https://leosheck.github.io/DMO_anti-VEGF_treatment_regimes/

## What the page shows

- Mean change in best-corrected visual acuity (ETDRS letters) against the number of injections given, at 1 year, 2 years, 3 years and 4 to 5 years.
- Vision change over time for each trial arm.
- A table of every trial arm and time point, grouped by dosing strategy, with a source for every figure.
- Filters by drug and by dosing strategy.

## Dosing strategies covered

1. DRCR modified PRN: Protocols I, T (with its 5-year extension) and AC.
2. PRN with monthly visits in industry trials: RESTORE and RETAIN.
3. Fixed monthly dosing: RISE, RIDE, VIVID, VISTA and TREX-DME.
4. Fixed every 8 weeks after loading doses: VIVID, VISTA, YOSEMITE, RHINE, PHOTON, KESTREL and KITE.
5. Treat-and-extend or personalised treatment interval: RETAIN, TREX-DME, YOSEMITE, RHINE and RHONE-X.
6. Long fixed interval, shortened if the disease is active: aflibercept 8 mg (PHOTON) and brolucizumab (KESTREL and KITE).

VIOLET, which compared maintenance strategies after the first year of treatment, is shown separately.

## Sources and limits

- All figures come from articles indexed in PubMed, using the abstract or the open-access full text. Each figure links to its source on the page.
- Injection counts are reported differently across trials. DRCR papers give medians and industry papers give means. For VIVID, VISTA, RISE and RIDE, the counts are the numbers the protocol scheduled, not the numbers patients received.
- Some year-1 injection counts are derived values. These are marked on the page.
- The trials took patients with different starting vision. Comparisons between trials are descriptive and are not adjusted for these differences.

Figures are current to 4 October 2026.
